Home Studio

Home to
Naughty Boy.

Coach House Music Studios is the creative base of Naughty Boy (Shahid Khan) — one of the most celebrated British producers of his generation. Born and raised in Watford, he used £44,000 in TV game show winnings to build his first studio and launch a career that would go on to define a decade of UK music.

His debut album Hotel Cabana (2013) spawned the UK number-one single "La La La" featuring Sam Smith, and the top five hit "Runnin' (Lose It All)" featuring Beyoncé and Arrow Benjamin. He produced Emeli Sandé's landmark debut album Our Version of Events — one of the best-selling albums by a British female artist in the 21st century, spending over 10 weeks at number one on the UK Albums Chart.

His production credits span Sam Smith, Beyoncé, Leona Lewis, Cheryl, Jennifer Hudson, Tinie Tempah, Zayn, Rihanna, Wiz Khalifa, Ed Sheeran, Wretch 32, Professor Green, JLS, Alesha Dixon and more — making him one of the most versatile and in-demand producers to ever come out of the UK.
